🧠 Where Claude Absolutely Crushes It

If you write for a living (or want to), Claude is your person.

Long-form content, nuanced analysis, legal docs, research summaries — Claude handles all of it without losing the plot halfway through. Its 200,000-token context window means it can hold an entire novel in its head and still give you coherent feedback on chapter 12.

It also just... writes better. Less corporate-speak, more actual sentences. It won't agree with everything you say just to be polite — which sounds annoying until you realise you've been asking ChatGPT "is this good?" and it's been saying yes to everything.

Claude is the tool for:

  • Long blog posts and articles
  • Editing and proofreading big documents
  • Summarising research or lengthy PDFs
  • Writing that needs to actually sound human

🤖 Where ChatGPT Has the Edge

ChatGPT is the better choice if you live in the tech/coding world or need a Swiss Army knife of integrations.

Its plugin ecosystem is massive. You can connect it to your calendar, browse the web, generate images with DALL-E, and run code — all without leaving the chat. If you're building something, automating workflows, or need an AI that plays nice with other tools, ChatGPT's got more toys.

ChatGPT is the tool for:

  • Writing and debugging code
  • Image generation (DALL-E built in)
  • Connecting to third-party apps and plugins
  • Quick back-and-forth tasks that don't need depth

🎯 Which One Should YOU Use?

Use Claude if you're a writer, marketer, or anyone who needs long, high-quality output that actually sounds like a human wrote it.

Use ChatGPT if you're a developer, need image generation, or want an AI that connects to your other tools.

Can't decide? Most serious users end up using both — Claude for writing and thinking, ChatGPT for building and doing. Both have solid free plans, so try both and see which clicks.
