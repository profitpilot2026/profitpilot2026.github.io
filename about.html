What Profit Pilot Is

Independent analysis on technology, finance, and global politics. No sponsors. No agenda. No filler.

Profit Pilot started with a simple frustration: most news tells you what happened. Almost none of it tells you what it actually means, why it matters, or what to do with the information. This site exists to close that gap.

Every post is written to answer one question: so what? Not just what the Fed said, but what that means for your money. Not just that a company launched a new AI model, but what the business model behind it tells you about where the industry is going. Not just that a ceasefire was announced, but whether it's real or theatre.

The writing is opinionated because vague analysis is useless. If the evidence points clearly in one direction, we say so. If something is genuinely uncertain, we say that too.

On independence and affiliates

Profit Pilot is fully independent. There are no sponsored posts, no paid placements, and no advertisers who influence what gets covered or how.

Some articles contain affiliate links — primarily in the AI tools reviews. If you sign up for a paid tool through one of those links, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. We only link to tools we'd recommend regardless. You can read our full affiliate disclosure here.
