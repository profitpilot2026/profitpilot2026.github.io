🤔 Why Are We Even Comparing These?

At first glance, Glean and ChatGPT seem like totally different tools. One searches your internal company data. The other is a general-purpose AI assistant.

But in practice, both are answering the same question employees ask every day: "Where is that information and what does it mean?"

If you're evaluating AI tools for your team or business, understanding where each one fits is critical — because buying the wrong one wastes both money and adoption effort.

⚡ The 30-Second Verdict

Glean = finds answers inside your company (Slack, Drive, Notion, emails).

ChatGPT = answers everything outside your company.

Smart companies use both. They solve different problems.

🟣 Where Glean Wins

Glean is purpose-built for one thing: finding information that lives inside your organisation. Slack messages, Google Drive docs, Confluence pages, Jira tickets, emails — Glean indexes all of it and lets anyone search across everything at once.

For large teams drowning in internal knowledge, this is genuinely transformative. No more "does anyone know where that doc lives?" in Slack. No more asking three people to find a policy that was written two years ago.

  • Searches across all your company's connected tools simultaneously
  • Surfaces relevant docs, messages, and files you didn't know existed
  • Keeps all data private and internal — nothing leaves your org
  • Builds a knowledge graph of your company over time

🟢 Where ChatGPT Wins

ChatGPT wins everywhere Glean doesn't play. Writing, coding, analysis, brainstorming, research, summarisation — ChatGPT is the Swiss Army knife for getting actual work done.

It's also dramatically cheaper and faster to deploy. No IT team needed, no integration project, no enterprise contract. Sign up, start working.

  • Content creation, copywriting, and editing
  • Code writing and debugging
  • Research and summarisation of public information
  • Brainstorming and ideation
  • Available to individuals, not just enterprises

🎯 Which One Should YOU Use?

Use Glean if you're at a company with 50+ employees, scattered knowledge across many tools, and a real problem finding internal information. It's an enterprise buy — you'll need IT involvement and budget approval.

Use ChatGPT if you need an AI assistant for actual work tasks — writing, coding, analysis, research. Works for individuals and teams alike, no setup required.

If you're a solo creator or small team: ChatGPT (or Claude) is all you need. Glean is overkill until you're drowning in internal docs.
